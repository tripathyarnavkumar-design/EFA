Plain-English glossary & references

Factor – a hidden theme that makes several items move together. Loading – how strongly an item belongs to a factor (−1 to 1). Communality – share of an item’s variation explained by all factors. KMO / Bartlett – checks that your data is suitable for factor analysis. Cronbach’s α – how consistently the items of a factor measure the same thing.

Standards applied: Kaiser (1974) KMO; Bartlett (1954); Horn (1965) parallel analysis; Kaiser eigenvalue > 1 (shown for comparison only); Costello & Osborne (2005) for loadings ≥ .40, cross-loadings ≥ .32 and ≥ 3 items per factor; Hair et al. for sample size and communality; Nunnally (1978) for α ≥ .70. Extraction: principal axis factoring. Missing values: listwise deletion.
